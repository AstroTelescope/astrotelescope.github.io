Precision Planetarium and Mount Control

AstroTelescope delivers a seamless planetarium and telescope control interface for astrophotography on macOS. Built as a front-end to INDIGO agents, it handles mount and auxiliary devices with effortless precision.

Interactive Star Mapping

Display star maps at any scale or rotation, with customizable density and labels. Access Hipparcos Catalogue and select NGC/IC deep-sky objects locally. Search any celestial body via SIMBAD online service.

Effortless Mount Synchronization

Control your mount by selecting objects on the map or manually. Sync position automatically to the center of solved images from AstroImager. Coordinates dome, GPS, and joystick drivers across the INDIGO bus.

Comprehensive Device Support

Out-of-the-box drivers for mounts like LX200, NexStar, iOptron, Takahashi Temma, and more. Includes PegasusAstro, Baader domes, powerboxes, flat panels, SQM meters, and weather sensors—all via INDIGO or third-party INDI.

Updated INDIGO Integration

Powered by INDIGO 2.0-300 for reliable performance. Connects to simulators, GPS daemons, HID joysticks, and specialized controllers like NexDome, SkyRoof, and Lunatico devices.

How It Works

1. Connect Your Gear

Launch AstroTelescope and link your mount, dome, GPS, or auxiliaries via built-in INDIGO drivers or third-party INDI servers.

2. Explore the Sky

View interactive star maps, adjust scale and rotation, and search objects locally or via SIMBAD for precise coordinates.

3. Slew and Sync

Select targets on the map or sync mount to AstroImager's solved field. All devices operate in unison on the INDIGO bus.

4. Capture Astrophotography

Monitor sky quality, dew points, and flats while your setup tracks flawlessly—ready for long-exposure imaging.

FAQ

Is AstroTelescope compatible with my mount?

Yes, it supports LX200 dialects (Meade, Losmandy, 10micron), NexStar, iOptron, SynScan, Takahashi Temma, and more via INDIGO drivers.

Does it require external services?

Core catalogs are local (Hipparcos, select NGC/IC). Online SIMBAD lookup is optional for rare objects.

What about domes and accessories?

Full support for Baader, NexDome, PegasusAstro, Lunatico powerboxes, flat panels, SQM meters, and cloud sensors.
